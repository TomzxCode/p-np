# Frontiers monitor: algorithmic, magnification, algebraic, certification (2026-10-03)

Scope: screens for 2025-2026 movement on (1) circuit lower bounds beyond TRC, (2) hardness
magnification/minicircuits, (3) algebraic complexity, (4) certificate/algebraic-certification
transitions, against the trigger lists in `williams_ladder.md`, `magnification_gap.md`,
`algebraic_rung.md`. The proof-complexity/Res(+o+) lane and the claim wave are NOT covered here
(separate agent). Method: arXiv API abstract pages, ECCC 2026 year listing (all 224 reports
scanned, Sept-Oct windows read directly), STOC 2026 / FOCS 2025 / FOCS 2026 official listings,
OpenAlex citation records, targeted websearch. No corpus files were edited; LOG.md untouched.

Headline: **no trigger fired and no named wall was weakened on any of the four fronts.**
The four most substantive finds are: the Dev Nag wire-accounting line (ECCC TR26-167, with a
board-flagged LLM-text episode and a 7/2-wire successor), the Ren-Williams FOCS 2026
near-maximum MA-query lower bound, the Raz FOCS 2026 non-commutative circuit record, and the
Kumar-Volk quadratic determinantal-complexity bound that supersedes an AI-written claim.

Terminology note: the brief's "CSE-vs-NSE (compression vs estimation)", "MCSP-Strategy", and
"PARITY-NSE" are not standard literature terms and appear in the corpus only as the parent's own
dispatch label (LOG.md line 1020). They were operationalized here as: (a) the two Williams-method
engines, compression (Circuit-SAT/C-type algorithms) vs estimation (CAPP), screened separately;
(b) the constructivization wall for MCSP-type problems (the `magnification_gap.md` porting
blocker, its Student-Teacher/refuter form in Carmosino-Grosser TR25-045); (c) the proven-sharp
2*eps magnification wall. Nothing found weakens any of these.

## Front 1: circuit lower bounds beyond TRC (the ladder)

**Status of the anchor result.** Chen-Tal-Wang, "Superquadratic Lower Bounds for Depth-2 Linear
Threshold Circuits" (ECCC TR26-039) is now formally published at STOC 2026, proceedings pp.
874-885, https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3798129.3800802 (slides:
https://wyc908.github.io/CTW26-slides.pdf). OpenAlex record W7164039064 shows cited_by_count = 0
as of today (https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=title.search:Superquadratic%20Lower%20Bounds%20for%20Depth-2%20Linear%20Threshold%20Circuits).
No successor paper improving the n^{2.5-eps} gate exponent was found in the ECCC 2026 listing,
arXiv cs.CC, or the FOCS 2026 accepted list.
**NO CHANGE** on ladder trigger 1 (no 2.5 -> 3 exponent step, no removal of the size-dependence
in the estimation runtime) and on trigger 3 (no OR/AND-closure result for THR o THR).

1. Dev Nag, "Almost-Everywhere Near-Cubic Wire Lower Bounds for SYM o THR and THR o THR",
   ECCC TR26-167 (6 Sep 2026; revision 1, 25 Sep 2026; 522 downloads),
   https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/167/.
   Statement: for every fixed c > 0 one language in E^NP fails agreement 1/2 + n^{-c} with
   every SYM o THR circuit of b n^3/log^10 n wires and every THR o THR circuit of
   b n^3/log^12 n wires, at every sufficiently large n (near-cubic WIRES, almost everywhere;
   prior wire record was Kane-Williams' n^{5/2}/polylog average case, arXiv:1511.07860).
   Status (verified from the report page): Oded Goldreich's Comment #1 (9 Sep 2026) records
   (i) that the board judges the v1 text "entirely generated by LLMs without much human effort"
   and has amended the CFP to prevent this, and (ii) that the core "seems to be a
   reinterpretation" of Chen-Tal-Wang counting wires instead of gates; the author's response
   (27 Sep 2026) supplies v2 (Zenodo 22773981, credits CTW, "a change of accounting, not of
   architecture"), a Lean 4 derivation of Theorem 2.5 from nine verbatim-quoted statements of
   the cited papers (github.com/devnag/near-cubic-wires), and a successor claiming
   n^{7/2}/polylog(n) wire bounds whose stated gate corollary (n^{2.5}/polylog) matches, and
   does not exceed, the CTW gate record (Zenodo 22774131).
   Verdict: **NEW CONTEXT, NO CHANGE.** Wire measure, not gate measure: any s-gate THR o THR
   circuit has at most s(n+1) wires, so even a true n^3/log^12 wire bound transfers to only
   ~n^2/log^12 gates, weaker than the known n^{2.5-eps} gate record. It is a wire-measure
   record claim built on the CTW framework, not a movement on any ladder trigger. Flag for the
   claim-wave ledger agent: the paper is ECCC-listed with expert scrutiny attached, and its
   machine checking is quote-conditional derivation, not independent verification; the same
   LLM-authored-text red flag the corpus tracks (failure modes) is documented by a board member
   on the record.
2. Hanlin Ren and Ryan Williams, "Near-Maximum Circuit Lower Bounds for Exponential Time with
   Merlin-Arthur Queries", ECCC TR26-118 (10 Jul 2026), accepted FOCS 2026,
   https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/118/ (FOCS listing:
   https://focs.computer.org/2026/accepted-papers/).
   Statement: E^{prMA}/1 (exponential time with a promise-MA oracle and one advice bit)
   requires circuits of size 2^n/n - o(2^n/n), via the iterative win-win paradigm and
   Range-Avoidance-to-lower-bounds reductions. **NEW CONTEXT**: largest-scale general-circuit
   lower bound for a strengthened exponential class; no named ladder trigger touched (no NP
   or THR o THR consequence, and it inherits rather than crosses the oracle-advice gap).
3. Shuichi Hirahara and Rahul Ilango, "NP-hardness of the Minimum Circuit Size Problem from
   Well-Studied Assumptions", FOCS 2025, pp. 1648-1664,
   https://focs.computer.org/2025/accepted-papers/ (proceedings page:
   https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/RecentIssue.jsp?punumber=; author copy:
   https://rahulilango.com/).
   Statement: MCSP is NP-hard under deterministic quasi-polynomial-time nonadaptive reductions
   assuming subexponentially-secure NIWI for SAT, subexponential non-deterministic circuit
   lower bounds for coNP, and P^NP/poly requiring size Omega(2^n/n); first evidence that MCSP
   is not in coNP. Verdict: **NEW CONTEXT, NO CHANGE** on the magnification constructivization
   blocker: all three assumptions are conditional, and the `magnification_gap.md` prerequisite
   (any UNCONDITIONAL super-linear time or circuit lower bound for MCSP-type problems) remains
   unmet; the result strengthens the plausibility side only.
4. Marco Carmosino, Ngu Dang, Tim Jackman, "Constructive Separations from Gate Elimination",
   arXiv:2604.23958 (27 Apr 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23958, and "Convergent Gate
   Elimination and Constructive Circuit Lower Bounds", arXiv:2602.17942 (20 Feb 2026),
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17942.
   Statement: gate elimination arguments are converted into refuters (algorithms producing an
   error input of any too-small circuit) for XOR, multiplexer, and affine-disperser bounds of
   the Li-Yang 3.1n-o(n) type, via a convergent term-rewriting formalization of circuit
   simplification; the XOR result is strengthened to circuits exactly matching 3(n-1) gates.
   Verdict: **NEW CONTEXT** for ladder trigger 4 and the `magnification_gap.md`
   constructivization section: the B2 record stays 3.1n-o(n) (45-year stall intact), but the
   first constructive/refuter versions of gate-elimination bounds now exist, which is exactly
   the constructivity currency the CJSW program trades in. **NO CHANGE** on the wall itself.
5. Alexander Golovnev and Mohit Gurumukhani, "Sumset Structure in Local Computation", ECCC
   TR26-195 (19 Sep 2026), https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/195/.
   Statement: for ell-local decision trees (each query an ell-local function), sufficiently
   strong lower bounds imply new consequences for provable circuit lower bounds, formalizing a
   new rung in the sumset/Paturi-lineage pipeline toward general-circuit bounds.
   **NEW CONTEXT** (conditional pipeline step; no bound proved).

## Front 3: algebraic complexity (VP vs VNP, perm-vs-det, GCT, shifted partials)

Headline: **no named algebraic wall moved** (SPD no-go, GCT occurrence no-gos, padding
dilemma, depth-reduction chasm, Kush mABP barrier, algebraic natural proofs all unchanged),
but this was an active half-year for restricted-model records and for implication arrows into
VP vs VNP.

1. Mrinal Kumar and Ben Lee Volk, "A Quadratic Lower Bound on Determinantal Complexity", ECCC
   TR26-218 (28 Sep 2026), https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/218/.
   Statement: the power sum polynomial sum x_i^n has determinantal complexity Omega(n^2) over
   C, proved unconditionally, superseding Sheshadri's recent AI-assisted and AI-written claim
   of the same bound (the ECCC abstract says so explicitly). Verdict: **NEW CONTEXT, NO
   CHANGE** on the perm-vs-det walls (power sum is not the permanent; dc is the det-side
   model), but it is the first human-verified superlinear dc lower bound for an explicit
   family and a notable data point for the corpus's machine-assisted-proof ledger (a
   human proof retiring an AI proof of a frontier-adjacent statement).
2. Ran Raz, "Polynomial Lower Bounds for Arithmetic Circuits over Non-Commutative Rings",
   arXiv:2604.22006 (23 Apr 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22006; FOCS 2026 version titled
   "A Lower Bound for Non-Commutative Circuits" (https://focs.computer.org/2026/accepted-papers/).
   Statement: an explicit n-variate degree-n polynomial requires Omega(n^{1.5}) product gates
   in non-commutative arithmetic circuits over every field (Omega(d sqrt(n)) at degree d),
   improving the prior n log d / d log n records (Baur-Strassen, Nisan). **NEW CONTEXT, NO
   CHANGE**: a genuine record in a restricted (non-commutative) model, consistent with the
   algebraic_rung.md lesson that restriction enables separation; VP vs VNP untouched.
3. Anand Kumar Narayanan, "Arithmetic circuit lower bounds from sumset expansion",
   arXiv:2607.15848 (17 Jul 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15848; ECCC TR26-138,
   https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/138/.
   Statement: via monomial-coordinate elusive functions and Chebotarev's theorem, elusiveness
   reduces to iterated-sumset expansion; resolves Garg-Makam-Oliveira-Wigderson's open problem
   (explicit elusive curves of exponential degree) and quadratically improves Raz's superlinear
   circuit-size record for depths d = o(log n/log log n). **NEW CONTEXT, NO CHANGE**: a
   technique that is neither SPD nor occurrence-based, partially filling the open slot named in
   algebraic_rung.md's verdict, at depths far above the chasm; no VP-vs-VNP consequence.
4. Peter Burgisser, "Intractability of Hilbert's Nullstellensatz implies algebraic hardness of
   permanent", arXiv:2606.25121 (23 Jun 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25121.
   Statement: P_C != NP_C over the complex BSM model implies VP^0(u) != VNP^0(u) uniformly,
   and nonuniform P^0_C != NP^0_C implies VP^0 != VNP^0. **NEW CONTEXT, NO CHANGE**: an
   implication arrow between the two P-vs-NP conjectures, not a new lower bound; relevant to
   front 4 as the current live movement on the BSM/certificate side.
5. Benjamin Rossman and Davidson Zhu, "Multiquadratic Sum-of-Squares Lower Bounds Imply
   VNC^1 != VNP", arXiv:2512.01227 (1 Dec 2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01227.
   Statement: an n^{d-o(log d)} SoS-complexity lower bound for explicit d-multiquadratic
   polynomials, for any omega(1) <= d <= O(log n/log log n), would separate VNC^1 from VNP.
   **NEW CONTEXT** (conditional target-setting in the HWY lineage; no unconditional bound).
6. Ian Orzel, Srikanth Srinivasan, Sebastien Tavenas, Amir Yehudayoff, "The Algebraic Cost of a
   Boolean Sum", arXiv:2502.02442 (Feb 2025; v2 Jul 2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02442.
   Statement: of three properties sufficient for VNP-hardness, the permanent and determinant
   share two, and only the permanent has the third ("cost of a Boolean sum"), also in the
   border setting. **NEW CONTEXT, NO CHANGE**: sharpens why the VNP-completeness proof fails
   for det; not a lower-bound technique.
7. Michael A. Forbes, "Exponential lower bounds for depth-3 circuits in small characteristic",
   FOCS 2026, https://focs.computer.org/2026/accepted-papers/ (no arXiv version found as of
   today). Statement (title-level, per the official listing): exponential lower bounds for
   depth-3 arithmetic circuits in small characteristic, extending the NW95/LST depth-3 picture
   past the characteristic-0 caveat that `algebraic_rung.md` records. **NEW CONTEXT, NO
   CHANGE**: closes a known low-characteristic gap at bounded depth; the depth-reduction chasm
   still blocks any VP-vs-VNP route.
8. GCT status: no construction of occurrence obstructions and no new rank lower bounds for
