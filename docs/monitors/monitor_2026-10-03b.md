## 4. ECCC 2026 reports: FLAGGED (two missing corpus items in the Res(+o+) lane)

Source: https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/year/2026/ (all 224 reports scanned by keyword).
Newest report is TR26-224, dated 2 Oct 2026; nothing dated 3 Oct 2026 yet. No 2026 ECCC
report claims to resolve P vs NP (keyword sweep found only conditional "unless P=NP"
statements, none in our lane).

New to the corpus (both squarely in the AC0[2]-Frege / Res(+o+) BPHP program the corpus
tracks; recommend adding to `proof_complexity.md` walls-map at the orchestrator's discretion):

- TR26-007, 2 Jan 2026, Yaroslav Alekseev and Nikita Gaevoy, "New Polynomial-Depth
  Res(+o+) Lower Bounds", https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/007/. Introduces the
  Constrained Bit Pigeonhole Principle (CBPHP). Under a combinatorial assumption, any
  Res(+o+) proof of CBPHP of depth N^k requires exp(N^Omega(1)) size for every constant k;
  unconditionally the same for RevRes(+o+); unconditionally depth N^(2-eps) Res(+o+) proofs
  need exp(N^Omega(eps)). Likely failure-mode relevance: none (this is a genuine lower-bound
  result, conditional in part); it matters as program context, since Braun's unrestricted
  BPHP bound supersedes its depth-restricted regime.
- TR26-018, 12 Feb 2026, Dmitry Itsykson, Vladimir Podolskii, Alexander Shekhovtsov,
  "Resolution Width Lifts to Near-Quadratic-Depth Res(+o+) Size",
  https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/018/. Width-w CNFs lifted by a 1-stifling gadget
  force Res(+o+) depth Omega(w^2/log S) at size S and size Omega(w^2) unconditionally;
  includes polynomial-size formulas with resolution refutations but superpolynomial-size
  Res(+o+) refutations below depth o(n^2/log^4 n). Same status: genuine result, program
  context for the Braun bound.
- Adjacent, lower priority, also not in the corpus: TR26-078, 3 May 2026, de Rezende,
  Engstrom, Ghannane, Risse, "Superpolynomial Length Lower Bounds for Tree-Like Semantic
  Proof Systems with Bounded Line Size", https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/078/
  (tree-like bounded-line-size Frege, not DAG-like AC0[2]-Frege).
- Already covered by the corpus: TR26-055 (Davis-Robere Res(log), `proof_complexity.md`
  line 73) and TR26-196 (Li-Ren-Zhong demi-bits generator, = arXiv:2609.23228,
  `proof_complexity.md` lines 540-541).
- Related-work gap found via OpenAlex during the citation sweep: Byramji and Impagliazzo,
  "Lower Bounds for Bit Pigeonhole Principles in Bounded-Depth Resolution over Parities",
  arXiv:2511.20023, 25 Nov 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20023 (depth N^(2-eps)
  exponential bounds for BPHP_n^(n+1) and t-collision generalizations). This is the
  "[BI, arXiv'25]" cited by TR26-018 and it is absent from the corpus.

## 5. New claimed P vs NP resolutions, 2025-2026, not in the corpus: FLAGGED

Corpus exclusions applied first (`failure_modes.md`): Arthanari 2606.03194, Goertzel
2510.08814, topology 2603.22211, graph framework 2508.13166, Xu-Zhou, Deng, Petros,
Trisduction; also McCallum 2005.10080 already noted in `clues.md` line 207.

New claims and claim-adjacent items:

- Edwards, arXiv:2512.11820, "Toward P vs NP: An Observer-Theoretic Separation via SPDP
  Rank and a ZFC-Equivalent Foundation within the N-Frame Model", v1 30 Nov 2025 through v5
  8 Jan 2026, 208 pages, https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11820. Claim: P != NP, via compilation
  of polynomial-time computation into sum-of-squares polynomials with polylogarithmic
  "contextual entanglement width", an NP-side rank lower bound on Ramanujan-Tseitin
  expanders, and a "rank-monotone extraction map" joining them; claims barrier immunity;
  machine-checked formalization "left as future work". Likely failure mode: F1 (the
  P-side/NP-side statements live in a bespoke SPDP-rank/CEW surrogate, and "bounded-CEW
  observers = P" is exactly the unproved normal-form-over-all-algorithms move) plus F5 (the
  P-side upper bound and NP-side lower bound are bridged by an extraction map whose
  both-directions proof is the whole problem). Version churn (5 versions in 6 weeks) and
  the missing formalization match known red flags. Highest-priority new item for the
  orchestrator.
- arXiv:2604.07406 (Apr 2026), claims the standard verifier-based definition of NP is
  unsatisfiable because it instantiates Hilbert's triple with Goedel's polynomial-time
  proof-checking relation, https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07406. Not a separation proof but a
  well-posedness attack. Likely failure mode: F1-adjacent (redefinition of the target: the
  argument turns the existential quantifier over verifiers into a requirement that bounded
  provability capture truth, which the standard definition never asserts). Flag for the
  claim-wave record.
- "P != NP under Closure of the Saturated SAT Layer", Zenodo record 20713602, 17 Jun 2026,
  mirror of arXiv:2602.00134, https://zenodo.org/records/20713602. Claim: a conditional
  separation inside "Six Birds Theory", contingent on a structural hypothesis
  Gamma_CSL-SAT-hidden; the text itself disclaims an unconditional ZFC separation. Likely
  failure mode: F5 by its own construction (the entire separation hangs on one unproved
  axiom-level hypothesis) with F1 scaffolding (typed carrier/instrument surrogate). Low
  priority because the claim is explicitly conditional.
- "P = NP by Exclusion of the SAT Separator Endpoint", Zenodo, 8 Jun 2026
  (indexed at https://exa.ai/library/publication/qgmr7klgpns). Claim: P = NP via an
  "AASC endpoint-rigidity" argument that the negative SAT branch is excluded, with Lean 4
  appendix support for a "local proof spine". Likely failure mode: F1 plus F6 (bespoke
  kernel/admissibility framework in place of a reduction, and Lean material verifying a
  local spine rather than the claim, the same pattern as the Arthanari audit). Rare P=NP
  direction, worth a line in the claim-wave ledger.
- "AETERNA P VS NP SINGULARITY ... Generalized Multiplicity Obstructions and Solution Tree
  Entropy Bounds", Zenodo, 11 Sep 2026, Dimitar Prodromov / AETERNA Technologies EOOD
  (indexed at https://exa.ai/library/publication/jgh2tpb6b7h). Claim: unconditional P != NP
  via explicit nonzero Kronecker-plethysm multiplicity representations in GCT plus
  "thermodynamic algorithmic entropy". Likely failure mode: F3 (asserts the GCT multiplicity
  obstructions whose construction is precisely the open problem, for permanent vs
  determinant) with F1 (entropy surrogate does the separation work). Grandiosity and
  licensing walls mark it low credibility; record to date the wave.
- "A Complete Proof of P != NP in ZFC and Its Qprime Formalization", academia.edu, posted
  around 4 Jan 2026, https://www.academia.edu/145775418. Claim: gap-free ZFC proof avoiding
  all three barriers, via "self-referential complexity" of an algorithm verifying its own
  states, plus a bespoke "Qprime" formalization. Likely failure mode: F1 plus the
  Petros-pattern barrier-dodging red flag (a diagonalization-style self-reference argument
  is exactly what the oracle worlds block). No community uptake found.
- Ramezanian, arXiv:2609.10864, 9 Sep 2026,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10864: explicitly NOT a resolution claim (self-described
  observer-relative unprovability theorem, not an unconditional P != NP). Note only, same
  genre as the Trisduction ledger entry.
